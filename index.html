Corporate HSE · Digital safety

Think Before You Post

A safety film for corporate HSE training on oversharing online. Rather than warn in the abstract, it shows the risk in the apps people use every day: a birthday, school and pet's name from a public profile fill in an account-recovery form, location tags show when a home sits empty, and a deleted post lives on in screenshots. It closes on four settings any employee can change in five minutes.

Script, UI design, motion design, animation

How a project runs

From footage to final

Step 1

Brief & selects

We agree the story and the audience. I log the footage and pull the moments worth keeping.

Step 2

Rough cut

The story in order, at the right length. You review structure before any polish.

Step 3

Picture lock

Pacing, graphics and motion are finished and timed to the frame.

Step 4

Sound & delivery

Mix, colour and loudness set for each platform, then exported in every format you need.
